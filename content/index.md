## Short Bio

I'm currently a PhD student at [School of Computer Science, Peking University](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/), under the supervision of Prof. [Yonghong Tian](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fn6hJx0AAAAJ). I closely collaborate with Dr. [Wei Fang](https://fangwei123456.github.io/) of MiroMind, and Prof. [Zhengyu Ma](https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=ZJQ5X9MAAAAJ) of Peng Cheng Laboratory. My research interests include spiking neural networks, AI infra, and AI for psychology.

* 2019.09 - 2023.07: bachelor of science, [School of Electronic Engineering and Computer Science, Peking University](https://eecs.pku.edu.cn/)
* 2020.09 - 2023.07: minor in psychology, [School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences, Peking University](https://www.psy.pku.edu.cn/)
* 2024.07 - 2025.07: visiting student, [Peng Cheng Laboratory](https://www.pcl.ac.cn/)
* 2023.09 - present: PhD student, [School of Computer Science, Peking University](https://cs.pku.edu.cn/)
